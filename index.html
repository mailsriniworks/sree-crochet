  1. 01

    Design & swatch

    Every piece starts as a pencil sketch and a gauge swatch. Stitch patterns are tested, frogged, and re-tested until the fabric drapes exactly right.

  2. 02

    Choose the yarn

    Merino, alpaca, linen, organic and recycled cotton — bought in small dye lots from mills that name their farms. Colors are chosen to age well, not trend well.

  3. 03

    Stitch by stitch

    The slow part. Cables, granny squares, pineapple lace — each product page lists the exact stitch pattern and hook size, because craft deserves credit.

  4. 04

    Block, finish, send

    Wet-blocked to shape, ends woven in by hand, wrapped in tissue and paper tape — never plastic — with a handwritten care card in every box.
